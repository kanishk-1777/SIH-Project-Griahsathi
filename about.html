About Griahsathi

Making reliable help easier to find.

Griahsathi is a service marketplace designed to connect people with dependable semi-skilled workers for everyday needs — from home maintenance and repairs to cleaning, shifting, agriculture and more.

Our vision

We want everyday services to be as easy to access as ordering anything online — while creating a dependable digital marketplace where local workers can find meaningful opportunities and customers can get work done with confidence.
